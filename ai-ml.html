★ SONG RECOMMENDATION ENGINE

A content-based music recommender built with Streamlit. You pick a genre and a release-year range, then dial in audio characteristics — acousticness, danceability, energy, instrumentalness, valence and tempo — and the app returns tracks that match your taste profile and embeds them for instant playback.

Problem it solves: Most discovery leans on popularity or listening history. This recommends by the actual sound of a track using a k-Nearest Neighbors model over Spotify audio-feature vectors, so you can surface songs that feel right even outside the mainstream.

★ MEDICAL SYMPTOMS CLASSIFIER

An NLP web app that reads a free-text description of how someone feels and classifies it into one of 25 ailment categories — for example head ache, back pain, cough, blurry vision or feeling dizzy.

Problem it solves: Patient descriptions are unstructured. Automatically categorising symptoms can support triage and make free-text healthcare data searchable and analysable. Text is cleaned with spaCy and NLTK, vectorised with TF-IDF, and classified with a scikit-learn model pipeline.

★ SIMPLE HAND TRACKING

Real-time hand tracking from a webcam feed — it detects hand landmarks and draws the hand skeleton live on every frame.

Problem it solves: Hand-landmark detection is the foundation for gesture control, sign-language recognition and AR interactions. This is a lightweight, real-time base for those, using MediaPipe's 21-point hand model on an OpenCV video stream.

★ VEHICLE DETECTION & COUNTING

A computer-vision pipeline that detects and counts vehicles moving along a highway from video, assigning each vehicle a persistent tracking ID.

Problem it solves: Automated vehicle counting supports traffic-flow analysis and smart-city monitoring without manual observation. Moving objects are isolated with MOG2 background subtraction, found via contour detection, and followed with a custom Euclidean-distance tracker.

★ CAR PARKING SPACE DETECTOR

Reads a parking-lot video and shows, in real time, how many spaces are free versus occupied, drawing a coloured box around each bay and a live free-count.

Problem it solves: Drivers waste time hunting for spots. Live occupancy detection is the core of smart-parking guidance — here each marked bay is analysed with adaptive thresholding and a pixel count to decide whether it is empty or taken.
